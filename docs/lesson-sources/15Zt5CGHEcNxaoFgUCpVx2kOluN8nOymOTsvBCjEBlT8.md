## **Present / Future simple tense (Ауыспалы осы/келер шақ)**

In a simple sentence the verb is ALWAYS in the end

## **Usage:** daily activities, near future.

  

## **Construction:**

  

all of the infinitive verbs end in -у / -ю

  

1.  verb stem\* + -а (-я), -е, -й (-и) + personal ending\*\*

 **-а (****я****)**: if the verb stem ends in a consonant and has strong (а, о, ұ, у, ы, я, и, ю) vowels in it

 **-е**: if the verb stem ends in a consonant and has soft (ә, ө, ү, і, е) vowels in it

 **-й (****и****)**: the verb stem ends in any vowel

 

  

бару (to go) → бар-а-мын = I go / will go

келу (to come) → кел-е-мін = 

жасау → жаса-й-

оқу (to read/study) → оқ-и-

қою (to put/stop) → қо-я-

  

\* verb stem = verb - y(ю)

\*\*personal endings for Present/Future Simple tense are following:

1.  to form a negation we add suffixes -май, -мей, -бай, -бей, -пай, -пей after the stem of the verb
      
    1.  Ex: іш-пей-мін, бар-май-мын - I don’t go, жаз-бай-мын; көрмеймін - I don’t see; істемеймін - I don’t do; тұрмаймын - I don’t live
          
        1.  Voiceless consonants (п, ф, с, ш, к, қ, т, ч, щ, ц, х, һ, б, в, ғ, г, д) + -пай, -пей

 

 жату - to lie down / жатпау - to not lie down

кесу - to cut / кеспеу - not cut

 кеспеймін - I don’t cut

  

 кету - to leave

 кетпеймін - I don’t leave
